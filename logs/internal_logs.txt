Type Promotion & Boolean Operations

1. Zen's numeric widening order is "byte → int → long → double".
2. Arithmetic between two "byte" values preserves "byte".
3. Arithmetic involving "byte" and "int" produces "int".
4. Arithmetic involving "int" and "long" produces "long".
5. Arithmetic involving "long" and "double" produces "double".
6. "bool" participates in binary arithmetic and is treated as "1" for "true" and "0" for "false".
7. "bool" also participates in binary bitwise operators ("&", "|", "^", "<<", ">>") using its "0"/"1" value.
8. Bitwise operations promote "byte" to "int"; "int" remains "int" and "long" remains "long".
9. Bitwise NOT ("~") does not accept "bool"; numeric "~" promotes "byte" to "int".
10. Logical and comparison operators produce "bool"; "!" operates on "bool" and returns "bool".
